Choosing the right network acceleration service requires understanding the physical routing differences between dedicated IPLC/IEPL circuits and BGP transit tunnels. This guide breaks down the essential criteria for selecting reliable providers and avoiding common industry traps.

## Physical Network Tiers

1. **IPLC / IEPL Dedicated Lines**: Enterprise-grade private connections that bypass the public international Internet gateway, offering zero packet loss and minimal jitter during peak hours.
2. **BGP Transit Tunnels**: Domestic multi-carrier entry points forwarding encrypted traffic through international subsea cables. Offers an excellent balance of bandwidth volume and pricing.
3. **Direct Public Routing**: Budget-friendly connections susceptible to evening congestion and jitter.
